Street Data Insight

ADDON. Version 1.1.1. Released on 8-Aug-2026. Provides 87 components. Created by @EDGARZR54.

Street Data Insight is an experimental toolset for urban planners and architects, providing custom components to reconstruct, visualize, and process street environments, including geolocation, geospatial orientation, and georeferenced operations on street networks and blocks, while leveraging panoramic and spatial imagery data, computer vision, and the capabilities of Rhino, Grasshopper, and related frameworks.

Este proyecto es de carácter académico y se ha utilizado material visual proveniente de Google Street View. Las imágenes empleadas son propiedad de Google y se incluyen exclusivamente con fines educativos e investigativos. Todos los derechos de las imágenes corresponden a Google. Año de publicación: 2025.
